The Gerry's 48-Hour Rain Spot & Satisfaction Guarantee

Minnesota weather can be unpredictable. If rain or sprinkler spray spots your glass within 48 hours of any scheduled service, contact us and a lead technician will return to touch up the affected panes completely free of charge. No hassle, no questions asked.

💧 0 PPM Pure DI Water Difference

Traditional cleaners use squeegees and dish soaps that leave an invisible, sticky microscopic film. This soap residue acts as a magnet for pollen, dirt, and dust, dulling your view within weeks.

We filter municipal water down to 0 PPM (parts per million) of dissolved minerals. When pure DI water dries on your glass, it leaves zero spots, zero streaks, and zero residue. Your windows stay clean twice as long.

🛡️ Ground-Based Carbon-Fiber Rig

We invest in commercial aerospace carbon-fiber water-fed pole systems. This enables our technicians to scrub and rinse 2nd and 3rd story glass safely from the ground.

Zero Ladders: No heavy aluminum ladders denting your aluminum gutters, scraping your siding, or damaging delicate Lake Minnetonka flowerbeds.
